## What round 2 must be

- **Breaks out of the grid.** Asymmetry, offsets, things that hang off the column, uneven rhythm between sections. Not every section built as label, heading, paragraph, cards.
- **Approachable, a little subversive, avant-garde, minimal, with a brutalist touch.** Raw structure shown on purpose (visible rules, system type, plain lists, exposed grids, hard edges) next to generous emptiness. Minimal means fewer things, not smaller things.
- **Editorial, boutique, specialist, creative.** A small studio with taste and a scene behind it, not an agency and not a conference.
- **Type with a point of view.** No generic serif and sans pairings and no stock condensed display (see the banned list). Giant type is allowed as a single moment on a page, not as every heading.
- **Colour:** keep the palette in `src/styles/tokens.css`, but use few of its colours. A **neutral ground** (not grey; see "Calm is not grey" in the skill), ink text, and **one or two bold colours for buttons** and a few moments. Series accents stay inside their projects.
- **Gradient:** a **grainy, abstract gradient**, subtle and full width. Build the grain without third-party requests (an inline SVG `feTurbulence` noise layer or a small self-hosted noise image). No waves, no wave-edge masks, no blobs with hard outlines.
- **No AI-generated design patterns** (see the checklist below). Patrick asked for a "taste skill" to be run for this; no such skill is installed in this project or account, so the checklist below stands in for it. Say so in the round notes.

## Type

- Self-host every face. Prefer `@fontsource/*` packages; libre faces from independent foundries (for example Velvetyne, Collletttivo, Use & Modify) may be self-hosted in `public/fonts/` if their licence allows it and the files can be downloaded here. Note the source and licence in the direction note.
- **Banned in round 2** (used in round 1, or stock defaults): Big Shoulders, Bebas Neue, Anton, Oswald, Archivo, Gloock, Bodoni Moda, Hanken Grotesk, Albert Sans, Manrope, Instrument Sans and Serif, Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Montserrat, Poppins, DM Sans and Serif, Space Grotesk, Playfair Display, Fraunces.
- A monospace or system-like face is welcome for the brutalist touch (labels, dates, lists), never for long text.
- The skill's type principles still apply: two families chosen for contrast, judged at their largest use, true styles only, real names must not break badly.

## Anti-AI checklist

Check every page against this before handing over, and note in the direction note which points you had to fix.

- Every section built as eyebrow label, big heading, paragraph, button.
- Three or four equal cards in a row, each with an icon, title and two lines.
- Bento grids of rounded cards with soft shadows; glass panels; a gradient blob behind a centred hero.
- Violet-to-blue gradients by default; gradient text.
- Pills on everything: pill tags, pill buttons, pill nav all at once.
- Uniform section rhythm: same padding, same alignment, everything centred.
- Stats counters, logo rows, testimonials with avatars, "How it works" steps, made-up numbers.
- Emoji or generic line icons as decoration.
- Copy that sounds like a template ("Elevate your", "Seamless", "Unforgettable experiences").

## How to build

1. Branch `design/r2-review` (this record branch, based on `claude/practical-curie-p8fa8n`) holds this brief and the logged verdicts. Commit the About content change there first.
2. Build three directions, each on its own branch from that commit: `design/r2-1-<name>`, `design/r2-2-<name>`, `design/r2-3-<name>`. Name them yourself.
3. Before building, write each direction's notes below (structural idea, references and moves taken, the section pattern table, the two type families with sources, palette roles).
4. Restyle through `src/styles/tokens.css`, `src/styles/base.css`, `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro`, `src/components/**` and the page markup in `src/pages/**`. Use the motion layer's attributes (`public/motion/motion.js`), not scroll JavaScript of your own.
5. `astro.config.mjs` keeps `devFeatures: { images: true, environmentVariables: false, edgeFunctions: false }` on the Netlify adapter (round 1 found that Edge Functions emulation fails on Patrick's Mac). It is already on the round 1 branches; add it if your base lacks it.
6. `npm run build`, serve the build, and loop `npm run design:check` until it is clean. A plain static server cannot answer the Netlify image URLs (`/.netlify/images?url=...`); `design/rounds/r1/serve.cjs` on `design/r1-review` can, and serves several builds from one process.
7. Look at your own renders at 375 and 1280 on every page type, including the sparse cases (`company-summer-party` has no events, `deep-listening-room` has no photos). Check hover, sticky and scroll states in a live Playwright session.

### 1. Run Sheet (`design/r2-1-run-sheet`)

- **Structural idea:** the site is set like a production document. A margin column carries mono marks (counts, dates), content hangs off it on visible hairlines, and the photo and portrait hang out of the column.
- **References and moves:** `list-group-names-hairline-plus` (names on hairlines with a plus, for the accordion) and `poster-future-boutique` (the programme as plain typography in a pale column). Does not reuse: round 1's rows-with-roles index, colour fields, gradients as grounds.
- **Type:** Happy Times at the IKOB (display and text, with its true italic) and Sligoil Micro (labels, dates, the fitted email). Both from Velvetyne, SIL OFL 1.1, self-hosted in `public/fonts/` with their licence files. Sources: `gitlab.com/velvetyne/Happy-Times-at-the-IKOB`, `gitlab.com/velvetyne/sligoil`.
- **Palette roles:** ground paper `#f2eee6` (sand lightened, warm), ink text, **red** `#f24a33` for buttons with ink text, yellow only as the marker on hover and selection. The grainy gradient is a full-width red-to-yellow haze behind the header that fades into the paper.
- **Anti-AI check:** the section label sits in the margin column and carries a count, so no section opens with an eyebrow over a heading. Left as is and worth a look: the About sections repeat one build (mark, italic heading, intro, list).

### 2. Grain (`design/r2-2-grain`)

- **Structural idea:** one grainy gradient is the site's weather. It fills the first screen, returns as the closing band and tints each project header in the series accent; between them the page is quiet, with narrow off-centre columns and things that straddle section edges.
- **References and moves:** the kept `r1-4` closing band and project header, and `hero-collective-split-photo` (unassigned in round 1: a small card docked into the corner of a large field). Does not reuse: wave edges, names scattered over the gradient, condensed capitals.
- **Type:** Basteleur (Moonlight for display, Bold for small headings; Velvetyne, SIL OFL 1.1, `gitlab.com/velvetyne/basteleur`, self-hosted in `public/fonts/`) and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text with Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for dates and footnote numerals (`@fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible-next`, `@fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible-mono`, SIL OFL).
- **Palette roles:** ground warm white `#faf7f2`, ink text, **violet** `#4c33f2` for buttons on the ground, an ink pill on the gradient fields, lilac and orchid only inside the gradient.
- **Anti-AI check, fixed:** the hero was moved off centre (text low and left, glow to the right) so it is not a blob behind a centred hero; the violet pill on the violet field was changed to ink; pills are kept to buttons and the kept project header, while nav and filters are plain text. Left as is: the closing band is centred heading, paragraph, button, because that is the kept section.

### 3. Blockwork (`design/r2-3-blockwork`)

- **Structural idea:** the grid is drawn. Sections are tables of cells that share one ink line, some filled with a photo or one flat colour, and blocks hang lower than their neighbours.
- **References and moves:** the kept `r1-3` pillar colour fields and `migas-alt-grid` / `cafe-menu-restaurant-retro` (drawn grids and menu tables). Does not reuse: stacked flyer sheets, bento cards with gaps and radii, poster capitals.
- **Type:** Sporting Grotesque (display and labels; Velvetyne, SIL OFL 1.1, `gitlab.com/velvetyne/Sporting-Grotesque`, converted from OTF to WOFF2 and self-hosted in `public/fonts/`) and Familjen Grotesk for text (`@fontsource/familjen-grotesk`, SIL OFL).
- **Palette roles:** ground sand `#e9e1d6`, ink text and lines, **yellow** `#f2d833` for buttons and filled cells with ink text, **violet** for the second pillar field, the closing band and the About process block. The grainy gradient is a full-width sand-to-lilac strip behind the hero headline and the Projects title.
- **Anti-AI check:** no pills anywhere; buttons, filters and nav items are cells. Left as is and worth a look: the company formats table is six equal cells (numbered, no icons, shared lines), which is the closest thing in the round to "equal cards in a row".
