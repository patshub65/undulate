**The visitor in ten seconds.** A company's event lead wants to know whether undulate can run their event, what kind of events those are, and who to write to. Someone from the scene wants to know what's coming up and what undulate has done. The best agencies and collectives in this field show their programme and their people, never stock photos. They let the artwork carry the colour, and they say plainly what they do.

**Rule for the round.** Each direction combines at least two references from different styles and names the move it takes from each. No two directions take the same element from the same reference. Each direction has one structural idea under a hero that speaks to companies.
