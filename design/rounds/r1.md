## The visitor in ten seconds

- **A company's event lead** wants to know whether undulate can run their event, what kind of events those are, and who to write to.
- **Someone from the scene** wants to know what's coming up and what undulate has done.

The best collectives, agencies and labels in this field show their programme and their people, never stock photos. They let the artwork carry the colour, and they say plainly what they do. undulate's selling point is subcultural credibility brought to companies: the hero's words address companies, while its imagery comes from her events.

## Rules for the round

- Each direction combines **at least two references from different styles** and names the move it takes from each. No two directions take the same element from the same reference.
- Each has **one structural idea**, placed under a hero that speaks to companies. The hero comes first, and the idea never replaces it.
- **Series artwork test:** `poster-bananas-are-berries` and `poster-future-boutique` are real undulate series flyers, and both are in the content as event flyers (`/projects/bananas-are-berries/`, `/projects/future-boutique/`). Every direction must hold artwork like it (and like the Future Boutique poster) without clashing: the frame stays quiet where the flyers are loud. The series accents come from their posters: *bananas are berries* `#f587a5`, *Future Boutique* `#901b2d`.
- **Directions differ in structure, not in colour and type.**
- Keep the content as it is. Don't edit `src/content/**`, `keystatic.config.ts`, `src/content.config.ts` or `src/lib/content.ts`. Copy shown on the page comes from content; a direction changes how it looks, not what it says.

## How to build

1. Start every direction from `claude/practical-curie-p8fa8n` on its own branch, as in the table. **Patrick has authorised pushing these five `design/*` branches and the round's record branch.** Don't push to `main` or merge anything.
2. Run `npm install`. Restyle through `src/styles/tokens.css`, then `src/styles/base.css`, `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro` and `src/components/**`. Use the motion layer's attributes (`public/motion/motion.js`) rather than writing scroll JavaScript of your own.
3. **Fonts: self-host from the start** with `@fontsource/*` npm packages, imported in the layout. Note the package names in the direction note. No Google Fonts links: the environment's network may block them, and the site must not call them anyway.
4. Replace the placeholder wordmark styling and favicon with something the direction owns. The logo itself is still a draft, so set "undulate" in the direction's display face.
5. Run `npm run build`, then serve the static build with `python3 -m http.server 4321 -d dist`. Run `npm run design:check` and loop until it is clean. The Netlify adapter's `astro preview` does not start in this environment.
6. Look at your own renders at 375 and 1280, on every page type, including the sparse cases: `company-summer-party` has no events, and `deep-listening-room` has no photos. Check hover, sticky and scroll states in a live Playwright session, not only in full-page captures.

### 1 · After Dark (`design/1-after-dark`)

1. **Structural idea:** a year as a giant numeral on the ink screen with that year's flyers sliding across its foot as an uneven filmstrip (`data-hscroll`). It carries "Coming up" on the homepage and the events on every project page, one numeral per year.
2. **References:** `hero-giant-year-filmstrip` (the giant numeral, cards at uneven sizes across its foot) + `listing-shows-grid-genre-tags` (projects as a programme grid with outline pill tags; the yellow "coming up" ticker under the header on every page).
3. **Not reused:** hairline rows with a roles column (2), colour fields and stacked sheets (3), gradient fields with names (4), chapter rows and photo-led pages (5).
4. **Type:** Big Shoulders Display 700/900 (`@fontsource/big-shoulders-display`) for display, Instrument Sans 400/500/600 (`@fontsource/instrument-sans`) for text. **Palette roles:** ink ground, off-white text, mint for buttons and the Companies field, yellow for the ticker and focus, violet-to-lilac gradient in the hero only. Phone nav: a "Menu" button opening a full-screen native popover.

### 2 · Gallery Sand (`design/2-gallery-sand`)

1. **Structural idea:** projects as an index of hairline rows, image and name on the left and the specialties as a muted column on the right. The same rows carry "Selected projects" on the homepage, and the project page lists its services the same way.
2. **References:** `index-project-rows-roles` (rows with the roles column) + `about-founder-split-pill-nav` (portrait beside a very large name for the About teaser and page; the pill nav docked at the thumb on phones) + far pick `hero-type-scattered-images-rails` (the headline mixes a light sans and a bold serif per word; joining words stay light, by rule, so it works on any headline Jasmin writes).
3. **Not reused:** the numeral and filmstrip (1), colour-blocked pillars and the programme (3), gradient fields (4), chapter covers and text columns (5).
4. **Type:** Gloock 400 (`@fontsource/gloock`) for display, Hanken Grotesk 300/400/500/600 (`@fontsource/hanken-grotesk`) for text. **Palette roles:** sand ground, ink text, violet as the single accent (primary button, links, the Companies field). Series accents show as a dot only.
   **The dock and the check:** a fixed dock passes over text while scrolling, which the overlap check would flag if text sat under it at load. On phones every page therefore opens on a title screen that keeps its foot clear (the hero photo, a tall page head, the portrait). In full-page screenshots the dock appears once, mid-page, where the first screen ends.

### 3 · Poster (`design/3-poster`)

1. **Structural idea:** each section reads as a flyer. The pillars are two uneven colour fields holding their specialties (yellow for Production, violet for Curation), events are stacked flyer sheets, and the project header is a field in the project's own colour.
2. **References:** `events-past-flyers-stacked` (a tinted sheet offset behind each flyer; the tint is the series accent where there is one) + `services-bento-cards` (uneven cards giving the two pillars weight) + `poster-future-boutique` (the programme in tall condensed capitals in two colours: used for the company formats, names in red with slider marks as dividers, and for a project's services, category in ink and names in red).
3. **Not reused:** the numeral and filmstrip (1), hairline rows and the dock (2), gradient fields and scattered names (4), chapter rows (5).
4. **Type:** Bebas Neue 400 (`@fontsource/bebas-neue`) for display, Archivo 400/500/700 (`@fontsource/archivo`) for text. **Palette roles:** white ground, ink text and buttons, yellow (Production, closing), violet (Curation, project call to action), red for names in the programme (large type only). The project header picks ink or white type from the accent's luminance, so the dark Future Boutique red takes white; a mid-tone accent that carries neither at AA (the sample blue of Deep Listening Room) is deepened and takes white. Phone nav: an ink band under the wordmark.

### 4 · Gradient Wave (`design/4-gradient-wave`)

1. **Structural idea:** one radial logo gradient (teal to mint, fading to the white frame) as the hero ground, with the company event formats and the projects scattered across it as names under the headline; stacked and centred on phones. The names link to the Companies section and the project pages.
2. **References:** `hero-roster-names-gradient` (names over a radial field) + `hero-gradient-condensed-type` (Companies as a red-to-pink gradient band with heavy condensed capitals) + `about-card-over-giant-type` (About as a paper card over giant cropped capitals).
3. **Not reused:** the numeral and filmstrip (1), hairline rows (2), flat colour blocks and stacked sheets (3), chapter rows (5).
4. **Type:** Anton 400 (`@fontsource/anton`) for display, Manrope 400/500/800 (`@fontsource/manrope`) for text and the names. **Palette roles:** white frame, ink text and buttons; gradients as fields with a wave-shaped edge (a CSS mask): mint-teal (hero, index head), red-pink (Companies, project call to action), dusk-orchid (Coming up), violet-lilac (closing, About). Body text on a field is ink where the field is light and white only where it is dark. The project header is a radial field mixed from the series accent. Phone nav: a "Menu" popover on the hero field.

### 5 · Editorial (`design/5-editorial`)

1. **Structural idea:** projects as magazine chapters, full-width rows between rules: a numbered cover square, a bold heading, the list of specialties, the summary and a column of facts.
2. **References:** `index-chapter-rows` (the chapter rows) + `profile-two-photos-text-column` (project page: tall images running to the frame beside a narrow text column with plain headings; a series leads with its latest flyers, a case study with its cover and first photo) + `about-text-over-portrait` (About: text over a full-bleed portrait).
3. **Not reused:** the numeral and filmstrip (1), the roles column and the dock (2), colour fields (3), gradient fields (4).
4. **Type:** Bodoni Moda 400, 400 italic, 700 (`@fontsource/bodoni-moda`) for display, Albert Sans 400/500/700 (`@fontsource/albert-sans`) for text. **Palette roles:** white ground, ink text and rules (one weight, 2px), dusk for buttons, links, numerals and the Companies spread. Phone nav: the masthead row between rules.
