## What Patrick said about Grain

- **The minimalism is good.** Keep it.
- **The headline font is good** (Basteleur Moonlight), but he would like to see other options too.
- **Gradients should fade instead of ending in a clean cut.** Today the gradient fields (hero, closing band, project header) stop with a hard edge. They should melt into the ground.
- **A bit more use of colour accents.**
- **An off-white ground.** The current `#faf7f2` reads as white. Go further towards paper.
- **Maybe more subtle grainy gradients.** These are quieter and can appear in more places, not louder ones.

## The reference: what to take, what to leave

`minimal-collective.md` describes a black, colourless, single-weight editorial site. Take the **discipline**, not the look:

- **Take:**
  - Restraint: every element earns its place.
  - Hierarchy from scale and tracking rather than weight.
  - Tight negative tracking on large type.
  - Hairlines as the structural language.
  - Generous gaps between sections (whitespace does the work of dividers).
  - Small ghost labels with a slight radius, used as taxonomy rather than as buttons.
  - Images at 0 radius with no shadow, laid as an **overlapping, asymmetric mosaic** instead of a grid.
  - Text overlapping an image in the hero.
  - Left-aligned editorial copy.
- **Leave:**
  - The black ground. Patrick wants off-white.
  - "No chromatic colour". Patrick wants more colour accents.
  - "No gradients". Grain is built on them.
  - "No filled buttons". undulate keeps one solid primary per screen (the company enquiry).
  - PolySans, which is commercial and can't be self-hosted, and its substitutes Inter and General Sans. Inter is banned, so stay off that family.
  - Its odd spacing values. Stay on the 8pt grid.

## The two variants

Both start from `design/r2-2-grain` and keep its structure: one grainy gradient as the site's weather, quiet pages between, narrow off-centre columns, and things straddling section edges. Both apply all of Patrick's points above. They differ in these ways:

| | Variant A | Variant B |
|---|---|---|
| **Display face** | Basteleur Moonlight kept, refined (sizes, tracking, where giant type appears). | A different display face. Pick it from the type sheet below. Non-generic, self-hostable, and not on the round 2 ban list. |
| **Text face** | May stay Atkinson Hyperlegible Next or change. | A new pairing that fits the new display face. |
| **Accent use** | Accents as small, precise moments: rules, labels, link hovers, the active nav, dates. | Accents as soft colour inside more of the grain gradients (several faint fields across the page, each fading out). |
| **From the reference** | The hairline structure and ghost labels. | The overlapping image mosaic, and text overlapping the hero image. |

Pick the accents from the palette in `src/styles/tokens.css`. Use **two or three at most** besides ink and violet, and check contrast. Series accents still stay inside their projects.

Each variant gets a short name: `design/r3-a-<name>`, `design/r3-b-<name>`.

## Gradients that fade

- No gradient field ends with a hard edge across the page. Fade the field into the ground: a mask (`mask-image: linear-gradient(...)`), radial falloff, or a long final stop in the ground colour. The grain fades with it.
- The grain stays subtle. Use one shared noise source (inline SVG `feTurbulence`, or a small self-hosted noise image) and no third-party requests.
- Check the text on every gradient for contrast at its weakest point, not only at its strongest.
- Check that the fade doesn't make content look washed out: text that sits where the gradient fades still needs AA.

## Unchanged

- **Content and structure.** The content model, content, `src/lib/content.ts`, routes, homepage order and behaviour hooks stay as they are on `design/r2-review`, including the About content model from round 2. A variant changes how things look, not what they say.
- **The About draft copy stays as is.** Jasmin's LinkedIn and Instagram could not be read in round 2.
- **Netlify adapter config.** `astro.config.mjs` keeps `devFeatures: { images: true, environmentVariables: false, edgeFunctions: false }`.
- **design-check.** `npm run design:check` must be clean for each variant.
- **Renders.** Look at your own renders at 375 and 1280 on every page type, including the sparse cases. Check hover, sticky and scroll states in a live Playwright session.
