## What Patrick said about Grain

- **The minimalism is good.** Keep it.
- **The headline font is good** (Basteleur Moonlight), but he would like to see other options too.
- **Gradients should fade instead of ending in a clean cut.** Today the gradient fields (hero, closing band, project header) stop with a hard edge. They should melt into the ground.
- **A bit more use of colour accents.**
- **An off-white ground.** The current `#faf7f2` reads as white. Go further towards paper.
- **Maybe more subtle grainy gradients.** These are quieter and can appear in more places, not louder ones.

## The reference: what to take, what to leave

`minimal-collective.md` describes a black, colourless, single-weight editorial site. Take the **discipline**, not the look:

- **Take:**
  - Restraint: every element earns its place.
  - Hierarchy from scale and tracking rather than weight.
  - Tight negative tracking on large type.
  - Hairlines as the structural language.
  - Generous gaps between sections (whitespace does the work of dividers).
  - Small ghost labels with a slight radius, used as taxonomy rather than as buttons.
  - Images at 0 radius with no shadow, laid as an **overlapping, asymmetric mosaic** instead of a grid.
  - Text overlapping an image in the hero.
  - Left-aligned editorial copy.
- **Leave:**
  - The black ground. Patrick wants off-white.
  - "No chromatic colour". Patrick wants more colour accents.
  - "No gradients". Grain is built on them.
  - "No filled buttons". undulate keeps one solid primary per screen (the company enquiry).
  - PolySans, which is commercial and can't be self-hosted, and its substitutes Inter and General Sans. Inter is banned, so stay off that family.
  - Its odd spacing values. Stay on the 8pt grid.

## The two variants

Both start from `design/r2-2-grain` and keep its structure: one grainy gradient as the site's weather, quiet pages between, narrow off-centre columns, and things straddling section edges. Both apply all of Patrick's points above. They differ in these ways:

| | Variant A | Variant B |
|---|---|---|
| **Display face** | Basteleur Moonlight kept, refined (sizes, tracking, where giant type appears). | A different display face. Pick it from the type sheet below. Non-generic, self-hostable, and not on the round 2 ban list. |
| **Text face** | May stay Atkinson Hyperlegible Next or change. | A new pairing that fits the new display face. |
| **Accent use** | Accents as small, precise moments: rules, labels, link hovers, the active nav, dates. | Accents as soft colour inside more of the grain gradients (several faint fields across the page, each fading out). |
| **From the reference** | The hairline structure and ghost labels. | The overlapping image mosaic, and text overlapping the hero image. |

Pick the accents from the palette in `src/styles/tokens.css`. Use **two or three at most** besides ink and violet, and check contrast. Series accents still stay inside their projects.

Each variant gets a short name: `design/r3-a-<name>`, `design/r3-b-<name>`.

## Gradients that fade

- No gradient field ends with a hard edge across the page. Fade the field into the ground: a mask (`mask-image: linear-gradient(...)`), radial falloff, or a long final stop in the ground colour. The grain fades with it.
- The grain stays subtle. Use one shared noise source (inline SVG `feTurbulence`, or a small self-hosted noise image) and no third-party requests.
- Check the text on every gradient for contrast at its weakest point, not only at its strongest.
- Check that the fade doesn't make content look washed out: text that sits where the gradient fades still needs AA.

## Unchanged

- **Content and structure.** The content model, content, `src/lib/content.ts`, routes, homepage order and behaviour hooks stay as they are on `design/r2-review`, including the About content model from round 2. A variant changes how things look, not what they say.
- **The About draft copy stays as is.** Jasmin's LinkedIn and Instagram could not be read in round 2.
- **Netlify adapter config.** `astro.config.mjs` keeps `devFeatures: { images: true, environmentVariables: false, edgeFunctions: false }`.
- **design-check.** `npm run design:check` must be clean for each variant.
- **Renders.** Look at your own renders at 375 and 1280 on every page type, including the sparse cases. Check hover, sticky and scroll states in a live Playwright session.

## Variant notes

**Status: built and delivered (2026-11-15). Waiting for Patrick's review.**

Both variants branch from `design/r2-2-grain` (`2486ec4`) and keep its structure: one grainy gradient as the site's weather, quiet pages between, narrow off-centre columns, things straddling section edges. Content, `src/lib/content.ts`, routes, homepage order, behaviour hooks and `astro.config.mjs` are untouched (`git diff 2486ec4 -- src/content src/lib keystatic.config.ts src/content.config.ts astro.config.mjs` is empty on both).

### Shared by both: the fade, the paper, the grain

- **How every fade is built.** A field no longer paints its own background. `.field` (in `src/styles/base.css`) draws the gradient in `::before` from a `--field` variable and the grain in `::after`, and both pseudo-elements take the same `mask-image: var(--fade)`. So the colour and its grain fade together, and the text inside stays unmasked at full strength.
  - `--fade-down` (hero, project header, projects index head, About head): solid to 45%, then a long falloff to nothing at the bottom. The field has no bottom edge; the paper takes over.
  - `--fade-both` (closing band, About contact): fades in from the paper above and out again below.
  - Radial masks (series tiles on About in both; the faint accent fields in B): the mask has the same shape as the colour, so a field ends where its colour ends, not at its box.
- **The grain** is still one inline SVG `feTurbulence` tile (`--grain` in `tokens.css`), no request. It is quieter than r2: finer (`baseFrequency` 0.9, 3 octaves) and at `opacity: 0.26` (A) and `0.24` (B) in `soft-light`, down from 0.55.
- **Paper ground:** A `#f0eadf`, B `#ede6da` (r2 was `#faf7f2`). Muted text is `#4d4a52` (6.7:1 on A's paper).
- **Field colours are softer:** the gradient stops are now alpha falloffs (`rgb(... / 0)`) rather than a solid lilac band, and the violet glow is kept away from text.
- **Contrast at the weakest point.** `field-contrast.txt` in each variant's folder: a script hides all text, takes a full-page screenshot and samples the ground under every line of text that sits on a field, at 375 and 1280, on seven pages, and compares it to that text's colour. A first run failed the dusk labels on the project headers (3.4 to 3.9:1) and the summary on Future Boutique's dark red accent; labels on a field are now ink and the accent mix is lighter (44% instead of 62%). What remains in B's file is the ink headline where the hero photo covers it: those letters are hidden under the photo and its white copy shows instead (see B).
- **Phone menu** is no longer white text on a violet-to-lilac field (white on lilac was 2.4:1). It is paper with the hero's haze fading in, links in ink.

### A: Hairline (`design/r3-a-hairline`)

- **What changed from Grain:** paper ground, fading fields, subtler grain (above); hairlines as the structure inside sections; ghost labels; red and dusk as small, precise accents; Basteleur refined.
- **Faces:** Basteleur Moonlight for display (Velvetyne, SIL OFL 1.1, `gitlab.com/velvetyne/basteleur`, self-hosted in `public/fonts/basteleur/`, unchanged from r2). Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for dates and footnote numerals (`@fontsource/*`, SIL OFL 1.1). Basteleur Bold is no longer used for the small kickers: those are now labels (Atkinson 600, 14px, tracked capitals).
- **Basteleur refined:** negative tracking that tightens with size (`--track-d` −0.02em, `--track-xl` −0.03em, from the reference); hero headline a little larger (up to 5.5rem); the pillar lines come down from the display size to `clamp(2rem … 4rem)`, so giant type stays a single moment per page: the closing heading in ink (`--d-xl`, up to 9rem) on the homepage, the title on project pages.
- **Palette roles:** ground paper `#f0eadf`, ink text, violet buttons on the ground, ink pills on the fields, lilac and orchid inside the fields only. Accents besides ink and violet:
  - **red** `#f24a33` for marks that are not text (3.1:1 on paper is too low for words): the active nav underline, link underlines (`.more`, prose links), the hover underline on nav and footer, the hairline that turns red over a company format on hover, the draft-note rule on About;
  - **dusk** `#5f559d` for small text: labels, dates, footnote numerals, the ghost label text (5.3:1 on paper).
- **From the reference:** hairlines as the structural language (footnotes on hairlines, each company format and About step opening on a hairline that closes at the end of the list, the filter lines on Projects, the footer's top line), and ghost labels with a 4px radius as taxonomy (the format on every project card, the specialties in the project header, which were round pills).
- **Fades:** hero and About head `--fade-down`; project header `--fade-down` in the series accent, with the cover photo pulled up into the fade; closing and About contact `--fade-both`, the violet held low behind the pill.

### B: Mosaic (`design/r3-b-mosaic`)

- **What changed from Grain:** paper ground, fading fields, subtler grain (above); a new type pairing; several faint accent fields across the page, each fading out; the headline running over the hero photo; an overlapping image mosaic for featured projects, the projects index and project photos. The featured drag strip is replaced by the mosaic.
- **Faces:** **Syne** for display, labels, buttons and nav (Bonjour Monde, made for the Synesthésies art centre; `@fontsource/syne`, SIL OFL 1.1), weight 400 for all headings, 500 for labels and buttons, 600 only for the wordmark. **Redaction** for text, with its true italic for the specialty lists (Jeremy Mickel and Forest Young; `@fontsource/redaction`, SIL OFL 1.1). Basteleur and Atkinson are removed from this branch.
- **Why Syne, from the type sheet:** it is the candidate furthest from Basteleur (a wide, flat-terminal grotesque against a calligraphic serif), so the two variants test a real alternative rather than a second serif. It takes the reference's discipline well: one weight, hierarchy from scale and tight tracking (−0.035em / −0.05em). It comes from an art-world identity, not a tech one, and its width keeps large lines from reading as condensed conference capitals. Redaction is a sharp text serif, so the pair has the contrast the skill asks for and none of the stock serif-and-sans pairings.
- **Palette roles:** ground paper `#ede6da`, ink text and labels, violet buttons on the ground, ink pills on the fields. Accents besides ink and violet, only as soft colour inside fields, never as text: **orchid** `#f2a0df`, **mint** `#33f5bc`, **yellow** `#f2d833`. Where they sit: hero (orchid top left, lilac top right, a breath of mint low left); pillars (orchid by the first pillar, yellow by the second); company formats (mint behind the pinned offer); coming up (yellow behind the next flyer); closing and About contact (lilac and orchid with mint at the far edge, violet low behind the pill); projects index head (lilac, orchid, mint). Mint and yellow never touch.
- **Fades:** the hero, project header, index head and About head use `--fade-down`; closing and contact `--fade-both`; the accent fields are radial, sized in rem and placed inside their section so each has faded to nothing before the section's edge.
- **From the reference:**
  - *Text overlapping the hero image.* The photo sits under the headline, pulled up by exactly one line on desktop and two on a phone, so its top meets a line boundary. A white copy of the headline (a CSS `::before` with `content: attr(data-text)`, `aria-hidden`, clipped by the photo, positioned with container-query units so it breaks on the same words) continues the headline over the photo. A 60% ink scrim lies under the copy only, which keeps white above 4.5:1 even on a white photo.
  - *The overlapping, asymmetric mosaic.* Photos at 0 radius, no shadow, overlapping by negative margins in groups of three (counted over visible cards, so the Projects filters keep the pattern; a card left alone at the end gets a wider place). Only photos overlap photos: each card's words keep to the part of its column no other photo reaches. Project photos step across and over each other the same way.
  - *Left-aligned editorial copy:* the closing band and About contact are left-aligned now (they were centred).
